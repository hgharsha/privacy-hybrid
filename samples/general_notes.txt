Trip planning notes -- Lisbon, spring (not personal)

Best time to visit: April to June, mild weather and fewer crowds than summer.
Neighborhoods: Alfama for old streets and fado, Baixa for shopping, Belem for the tower and pastries.
Transport: the 28 tram is scenic but crowded; buy a rechargeable day pass for metro, bus and trams.
Food to try: pasteis de nata, grilled sardines, bacalhau, ginjinha.
Day trips: Sintra (palaces, half a day), Cascais (beach, 40 min by train), Setubal (dolphins and wine).
Budget: about 120 euros per day covering a mid-range hotel, meals and transport.
Tip: many museums are closed on Mondays; check hours before going.
